02 // RESEARCH

Capsicum Leaf Disease Detection

Automated Diagnostic Systems for Smarter Agriculture

Capsicum (bell pepper) crops are highly susceptible to leaf diseases like bacterial spot, powdery mildew, and fungal blights. Delayed detection often causes severe crop damage and financial loss for smallholder farmers.

My final-year thesis research explores an automated system to detect and classify diseases in capsicum leaves using image processing and machine learning techniques. By analyzing visual leaf patterns, texture anomalies, and color spot distributions, the system isolates infected areas and provides early warning indicators.

Ultimate Goal: To help farmers identify leaf diseases at an early stage before infection spreads across entire crops, significantly reducing agricultural loss and advancing smart, data-driven farming practices.

Image Processing & Feature Extraction

Normalizing high-resolution leaf images, segmenting leaf contours, and extracting color-space texture signatures.

Machine Learning Classification

Training pattern recognition models to classify specific disease types early with robust accuracy metrics.

Agricultural Impact & Sustainability

Empowering local farmers with accessible diagnostic technology to optimize pesticide use and protect yields.
